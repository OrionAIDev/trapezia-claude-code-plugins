Your last message was a wall of text, unclear, or otherwise not landing. Stop and reset.

He's running multiple projects and many sessions in parallel — he does not have the context that you do, and he does not want to dig for it.

Rewrite your last point as a short update to a non-technical boss:

1. **What's going on** — 1-3 plain-English sentences, no jargon, no code, no file paths unless truly necessary. Say what project/thing this is about.
2. **The question** — if you need a decision, ask it as a single, concrete question a boss could answer without technical background. State each option as what it leads to, not how it works.
3. **Your recommendation** — one line: what you'd do and why, so he can just say "yes, do that" if he agrees.

Keep the whole thing short. If there's no open question, drop section 2 and just give the update + recommendation (or just the update, if no action is needed).
